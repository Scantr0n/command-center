costBasisUSD cost of actually acquiring this item (what it was paid for), a non-negative number or null. Drives real profit figures separate from the fee-only payout estimates below, leave null until you have the real number, never guess it.
categoryOptional eBay category classifier, either "shoes", "electronics", or omitted/null. "shoes" switches every eBay fee calculation on this page to the real 15.3% Clothing, Shoes & Accessories final value fee instead of the 13.6% standard rate, and controls which itemSpecifics fields are checked in "Needs backfill" below, since size and color only matter for something you actually wear.

listingUrlsOptional object keyed by platform (each key a subset of platforms), value the real live listing URL as a string once it's been copied over, or null until logged. Powers a direct "View listing" link per platform in the item detail view, never invent a URL, leave it null rather than guess.
statusOne of: draft, ready-to-post, live, sold
datePublishedISO date (YYYY-MM-DD) or null if not tracked yet. Also drives the "Relist reminders" section below, log the real date an item first went live to get relist guidance for it.
notesFree text, or null
locationReal physical storage spot for this item right now, e.g. "Bin 3" or "Closet shelf B", a string or null until logged. Standard reseller inventory practice: pairs a bin/shelf label with each item so it can actually be found and shipped fast once it sells, rather than searched for. Purely descriptive, doesn't drive any calculation on this page.

A live eBay listing with this left null, or set to anything mentioning parts/accessories/auto, gets flagged in "Needs backfill" below so that exact bug can't repeat unnoticed.

Price drop & markdown guidance, by platform
What actually happens when you lower the price on a stale, unsold listing, separate from the relist timing above (which is about republishing, not repricing). Sourced from each platform's own current tools and documented behavior as of September 2026. No platform-endorsed or reseller-community-verified markdown schedule (e.g. "10% off at 30 days") exists across any of these four platforms, so none is presented here as a rule to follow, only what each platform's own price-drop mechanics actually do.
Platform What actually happens when you drop the price Practical tip
eBay eBay piloted an automatic price-reduction feature for unsold fixed-price listings, but it rolled out inconsistently and was removed in February 2026, it isn't a dependable feature to plan around. The real, currently-live markdown tool is Markdown Manager (Seller Hub > Marketing > Promotions), which shows a strikethrough original/sale price and notifies watchers, but it requires an active eBay Store subscription. A plain price edit alone doesn't reset a listing's search "freshness", that decays with age regardless of price Without a Store subscription, there's no in-platform markdown tool, a manual price edit is the only lever and it won't refresh search placement by itself
Vinted No dedicated price-drop feature, sellers just edit the listing price directly, and doing so does not reset the listing's recency the way a full delete-and-relist does. It does trigger a real notification though: a price cut of roughly 5% or more pushes an alert to everyone who favorited the item A price cut under about 5% likely won't notify anyone who liked it, make the drop meaningful enough to clear that threshold if the goal is to re-surface the listing to past interest
Poshmark Confirmed mechanic: dropping the price at least 10% below the item's lowest historical price (on items priced $10+) auto-notifies everyone who liked it across newsfeed, push, and email. The separate "Offer to Likers" tool sends a targeted discounted offer directly to likers instead of just lowering the public price, and also requires at least a 10% discount to send Each further markdown round needs to beat the previous lowest price by 10%+ to trigger a new round of liker notifications, a smaller drop won't re-alert anyone
Depop Depop's bulk price-drop tool notifies everyone who liked or saved the item and adds a visible "Price Drop" badge to the listing. There's no platform-wide discount-code system for individual sellers, what Depop does offer natively is a bundle-discount tool (commonly 10-20% off for 2+ items bought together) For the 48 ready-to-post drafts once live, a bundle discount can move slow items faster than a solo price drop, pair the two rather than relying on either alone

Relevant to the softball swing analyzer, the one battery-powered item actually live right now, sourced from each platform's own current electronics/dangerous-goods policy and USPS's lithium battery mailing rules as of September 2026. Platform Can a battery-powered device be listed Real gotcha
eBay Yes USPS requires the "installed in equipment" battery, like the one in the swing analyzer, to ship differently from a loose spare: installed is mailable via air or ground in limited quantity, a loose spare battery not packed with its device is Ground-only. Poshmark Yes, with a data-wipe step A pre-owned electronic has to be logged out of and disconnected from any linked account or app, and factory-reset where that's possible, before it ships; for the swing analyzer specifically, unpair it from Jack's own phone and any swing-tracking app account first, the same class of "looks done, isn't" risk as the eBay return-policy bug logged above

Shipping cost reference: flat rate vs weight-based
Real USPS retail counter prices, effective July 12, 2026, per USPS's own published rate change and multiple current shipping-industry rate trackers as of September 2026. These are counter prices, a discounted label bought through eBay's, Vinted's, Poshmark's, or Depop's own label tool (or a service like Pirate Ship) usually runs below them, so treat this as a ceiling to sanity-check a label price against, not what a label will actually cost. Ground Advantage prices by actual weight and destination zone instead of a flat price, so no single dollar figure for it is accurate across every real package, weigh the actual packed box and check the real price at the label tool rather than guessing from a table here.
Priority Mail Flat Rate box Retail price Ships up to
Small Flat Rate Box $13.65 70 lbs, any US zone, same fixed price
Medium Flat Rate Box (either configuration) $24.80 70 lbs, any US zone, same fixed price
Large Flat Rate Box $34.00 70 lbs, any US zone, same fixed price
Which one is actually cheaper depends on the item, not a fixed rule: a flat rate box wins once a package is heavy or dense enough, or is going far enough (Ground Advantage's zone pricing climbs with distance, flat rate doesn't), that its fixed price undercuts what weight-and-zone pricing would charge for the same box. Relist reminders

Platform-aware guidance on when to refresh a live listing that hasn't sold. eBay, Vinted, and Depop's search all favor listing recency, common reseller practice is to relist or renew after about 30 days without a sale. Poshmark is the exception: its Excessive Listing Removal Policy blocks relisting the same item again before day 60, doing it earlier risks the listing getting pulled instead of refreshed. This needs a real datePublished logged per item to compute anything, it stays honestly blank until then, no guessed dates. No cross-listing tool is connected here to relist automatically, so Add to calendar exports the real upcoming due dates below as a downloadable .ics file, up to two events per item: one combined event for all non-Poshmark platforms it's live on together (they share the same 30-day guidance), and a separate one if it's on Poshmark, since that platform's 60-day hold window is a real, distinct deadline. Common seller scam patterns, by platform
Real fraud patterns actually documented in each platform's own help center or reported widely enough by sellers to be worth watching for, sourced as of September 2026. This is what to watch for before and after a sale, separate from the dispute mechanics table above, which covers what happens once a legitimate case is already open.
Platform Pattern How to protect against it
eBay Item-not-as-described (INAD) return fraud: a buyer claims the item doesn't match the listing to force a return or refund even when it does. This has overtaken item-not-received as the most damaging scam sellers face on eBay, since eBay's buyer-friendly return policy makes it easy to open. Photograph the item from multiple angles (and any serial number) and video yourself boxing it before shipping, then compare what comes back against those pre-ship photos before refunding a return, escalate to eBay if it doesn't match. Vinted Off-platform payment request: a buyer pushes to pay outside Vinted's own checkout (PayPal Friends & Family, Revolut, bank transfer, a "pay here" link), which forfeits Vinted Buyer/Seller Protection entirely since it only covers payments actually made through the app. Only ever accept payment through Vinted's own checkout, never off-app. Record sealing the box with the tracking label visible, so a later damaged or never-arrived claim has real evidence behind it.
Poshmark Same off-platform payment scam as Vinted: a buyer or seller pushes a deal to Venmo, Cash App, or similar, sometimes with a "no fees" or discount pitch, or a profile that says it doesn't sell through the app. Posh Protect only covers transactions made on Poshmark, so leaving the app loses that protection the moment payment moves off it. Sales tax: who actually collects it
Not tax advice. All four platforms are legally a "marketplace facilitator" in every US state that charges a sales tax, which means the platform itself, not the seller, is required to calculate, collect, and remit sales tax on a sale. That tax is added on top of the buyer's total at checkout; it is never deducted from the seller's payout, so it never shows up in this dashboard's fee math or net-payout figures, and it doesn't need to. This is separate from income tax: sales tax is the buyer's cost of purchase, income tax on real profit is still owed regardless of the 1099-K threshold below.
Platform Sales tax Note
eBay Collected & remitted by eBay Charged to the buyer at checkout on top of the item price and shipping
Poshmark Collected & remitted by Poshmark Same, added to the buyer's order total, not taken from the seller's earnings
Depop Collected & remitted by Depop Same
Vinted Collected & remitted by Vinted Same rule, worth a second look here since Vinted's US marketplace is newer than the other three's
The one real edge case: a marketplace facilitator only has to collect in a state once it crosses that state's own economic-nexus threshold, so a seller could in theory owe it themselves on a sale into a state a platform hasn't reached yet. All four of these platforms already do enough national volume to clear every state's threshold by a wide margin, so this isn't a live risk at this operation's size, only worth knowing about if that ever changes.
