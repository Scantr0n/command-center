Search & discovery, by platform
What actually drives a listing into a buyer's search results, sourced from each platform's own documented ranking behavior as of September 2026. Fitting a title under the character cap above is necessary but not sufficient, this is what to actually put in that title, the description, and the category fields to get found at all, worth reviewing before the 48 ready-to-post Depop drafts go live. Reference only, not computed from any logged data here.
Platform What actually ranks a listing Practical tip
eBay The Cassini search algorithm, on title keywords and the Item Specifics fields (brand, size, color, condition, MPN, etc.), not hashtags, eBay doesn't use them Fill in every real Item Specific field the listing form offers, not just the title, a title-only listing is invisible to a buyer filtering by size or brand
Vinted The catalog category itself is the primary discovery path, picking the most specific leaf category (e.g. Women > Shoes > Boots, not just Women > Shoes) matters more than free-text keywords Always drill into the deepest matching leaf category before publishing, a listing filed one level too shallow just doesn't surface in a buyer's narrower browse
Poshmark Title keywords and structured fields (brand, size, category) are weighted highest; hashtags in the description work only as secondary, long-tail keyword reinforcement Repeat the real brand and item type once in the description in plain language, on top of the title, before reaching for hashtags
Depop Up to 5 hashtags, treated as real searchable keywords, plus brand/description keyword matching; leading with a descriptive word like "vintage" early in the description helps the algorithm place it Mix a couple of broad hashtags with a couple of niche ones rather than 5 generic tags, and never tag something irrelevant to chase reach, Depop can reduce a listing's visibility for spammy tags

What actually keeps an already-published listing visible day to day, separate from the one-time setup in the search & discovery table above and the 30/60-day relist timing below. Sourced from each platform's own current seller guidance and documented 2026 algorithm changes; several old habits stopped working this year, worth knowing before sinking time into them across the 9 live listings.
Platform Ongoing engagement action What actually works in 2026 Practical tip
eBay Promoted Listings Standard, a cost-per-sale ad A percentage-based ad rate charged only when a promoted listing is clicked and bought within the attribution window, no sale means no charge; a competitive rate pushes a listing a few positions higher in search Start the ad rate low, around 2%, and raise it gradually based on real view/sale data rather than jumping straight to a double-digit rate

Poshmark Sharing a listing to followers and Posh Parties Poshmark removed the Bulk Sharing tool and the algorithm no longer rewards high-volume sharing the way it used to; manual sharing a couple times a day still has some value, but far less than before Put the time old-style constant sharing used to take into relisting stale 60+ day items instead, deleting and relisting one triggers the same new-listing boost as an initial post
Depop The in-app Refresh button As of 2026, Refresh no longer affects search ranking at all, only an actual new listing (or a full delete-and-relist) gets the freshness boost, and that wipes the listing's existing likes and saves Don't spend time refreshing; if a listing has genuinely stalled, weigh a real relist against losing its existing engagement rather than reaching for refresh out of habit
Price drop & markdown guidance, by platform
What actually happens when you lower the price on a stale, unsold listing, separate from the relist timing above (which is about republishing, not repricing). Sourced from each platform's own current tools and documented behavior as of September 2026. No platform-endorsed or reseller-community-verified markdown schedule (e.g. "10% off at 30 days") exists across any of these four platforms, so none is presented here as a rule to follow, only what each platform's own price-drop mechanics actually do.
Platform What actually happens when you drop the price Practical tip
eBay eBay piloted an automatic price-reduction feature for unsold fixed-price listings, but it rolled out inconsistently and was removed in February 2026, it isn't a dependable feature to plan around. The real, currently-live markdown tool is Markdown Manager (Seller Hub > Marketing > Promotions), which shows a strikethrough original/sale price and notifies watchers, but it requires an active eBay Store subscription. A plain price edit alone doesn't reset a listing's search "freshness", that decays with age regardless of price Without a Store subscription, there's no in-platform markdown tool, a manual price edit is the only lever and it won't refresh search placement by itself
Vinted No dedicated price-drop feature, sellers just edit the listing price directly, and doing so does not reset the listing's recency the way a full delete-and-relist does. It does trigger a real notification though: a price cut of roughly 5% or more pushes an alert to everyone who favorited the item A price cut under about 5% likely won't notify anyone who liked it, make the drop meaningful enough to clear that threshold if the goal is to re-surface the listing to past interest
Poshmark Confirmed mechanic: dropping the price at least 10% below the item's lowest historical price (on items priced $10+) auto-notifies everyone who liked it across newsfeed, push, and email. The separate "Offer to Likers" tool sends a targeted discounted offer directly to likers instead of just lowering the public price, and also requires at least a 10% discount to send Each further markdown round needs to beat the previous lowest price by 10%+ to trigger a new round of liker notifications, a smaller drop won't re-alert anyone
Depop Depop's bulk price-drop tool notifies everyone who liked or saved the item and adds a visible "Price Drop" badge to the listing. There's no platform-wide discount-code system for individual sellers, what Depop does offer natively is a bundle-discount tool (commonly 10-20% off for 2+ items bought together) For the 48 ready-to-post drafts once live, a bundle discount can move slow items faster than a solo price drop, pair the two rather than relying on either alone

Electronics & battery-item rules by platform
Relevant to the softball swing analyzer, the one battery-powered item actually live right now, sourced from each platform's own current electronics/dangerous-goods policy and USPS's lithium battery mailing rules as of September 2026. This is a separate concern from the packaging table above: that one governs the box, this one governs whether the item can be listed at all, and how it has to ship once it can.
Platform Can a battery-powered device be listed Real gotcha
eBay Yes USPS requires the "installed in equipment" battery, like the one in the swing analyzer, to ship differently from a loose spare: installed is mailable via air or ground in limited quantity, a loose spare battery not packed with its device is Ground-only. A pre-owned electronic containing a lithium battery is restricted to Ground Advantage, Retail Ground, or Parcel Select, no air/Priority service, and needs the current lithium battery mark on the label; the older mark without a phone number retires December 31, 2026
Vinted Yes, with limits Battery capacity is capped at 100 Wh, and a package can carry at most 2 batteries, with at most 2 battery-powered devices in one bundle; a battery that's damaged, swollen, corroding, or leaking can't be shipped at all, check it before packing, not just before listing
Poshmark Yes, with a data-wipe step A pre-owned electronic has to be logged out of and disconnected from any linked account or app, and factory-reset where that's possible, before it ships; for the swing analyzer specifically, unpair it from Jack's own phone and any swing-tracking app account first, the same class of "looks done, isn't" risk as the eBay return-policy bug logged above
Depop No, not allowed at all Depop bans any item that runs on electrical power, including batteries, with a narrow exception for cameras/camcorders; the swing analyzer doesn't qualify for that exception. Shipping cost reference: flat rate vs weight-based
Real USPS retail counter prices, effective July 12, 2026, per USPS's own published rate change and multiple current shipping-industry rate trackers as of September 2026. These are counter prices, a discounted label bought through eBay's, Vinted's, Poshmark's, or Depop's own label tool (or a service like Pirate Ship) usually runs below them, so treat this as a ceiling to sanity-check a label price against, not what a label will actually cost. Ground Advantage prices by actual weight and destination zone instead of a flat price, so no single dollar figure for it is accurate across every real package, weigh the actual packed box and check the real price at the label tool rather than guessing from a table here.
Priority Mail Flat Rate box Retail price Ships up to
Small Flat Rate Box $13.65 70 lbs, any US zone, same fixed price
Medium Flat Rate Box (either configuration) $24.80 70 lbs, any US zone, same fixed price
Large Flat Rate Box $34.00 70 lbs, any US zone, same fixed price
Which one is actually cheaper depends on the item, not a fixed rule: a flat rate box wins once a package is heavy or dense enough, or is going far enough (Ground Advantage's zone pricing climbs with distance, flat rate doesn't), that its fixed price undercuts what weight-and-zone pricing would charge for the same box. A light package going to a nearby zone is usually cheaper priced by actual weight on Ground Advantage instead. There's no fixed weight where that crossover happens, it moves with the destination zone, so the only reliable way to know which is cheaper for a specific real package is to price both at the actual label tool before buying either one.

Multi-item bundle discount

Real per-platform bundle mechanics, sourced from each platform's own current seller documentation as of September 2026. Poshmark: a seller-set Bundle Discount, a percentage plus a minimum item count set once under Account > My Seller Discount, applies automatically whenever a buyer checks out 2+ items from the same closet together; buyers also pay one flat $7.97 shipping charge per order either way as of 2026, so bundling turns that fixed shipping cost into a per-item win for the buyer even before any seller discount. Vinted: a Standard seller can set a closet-wide bundle discount that applies automatically on a 2+ item order, and the items ship on one combined postage label instead of separate ones, but Vinted charges the seller no fee at all, so a Vinted bundle discount is pure shipping convenience for the buyer, not a seller fee saving. eBay: nothing applies by itself, a seller sets Combined Shipping Discount rules under Shipping preferences that cut shipping cost on a multi-item order from one buyer; the same 13.6% final value fee documented above still applies per item, only the one $0.30/$0.40 per-order fee is shared once instead of paid per listing. Offer response guide

Real reseller negotiation practice, a counteroffer ladder: accept a near-target offer outright, counter a good-but-low one once, let a borderline offer's answer depend on how long the item's actually been listed (reusing the same 30-day freshness window as the relist guidance above), and decline a deep lowball without countering. This applies that framework to a real logged asking price, and the real cost basis if one's logged, it is not a live read of any actual buyer offer, there is no live account connection to any platform's offers inbox.
Offer mechanics by platform
eBayBest Offer. A seller's counteroffer is valid 96 hours (4 days) for the buyer to respond, per eBay's March 2026 change (previously 24 hours). A seller-initiated offer sent to a buyer is valid 96 hours or until the listing sells, whichever comes first.
VintedMake an offer. No expiration on either side, an offer stays open until it's explicitly accepted or declined, the only one of the four with no time limit.
PoshmarkAn Offer to Likers stays open 24 hours. A buyer-initiated offer lasts 48 hours or until the seller accepts, counters, or declines it, whichever comes first.
DepopA buyer's offer, or a seller's counter to one, gives the other side 24 hours to accept, decline, or counter again. An accepted offer can be locked in as binding on the buyer for 24 hours.

Logging a sale here is separate from marking that platform in the item's soldOn array above, a real sale needs both done, one to log the sale itself and one to flag that item for delisting elsewhere. Profit is only shown once a real costBasis or shippingCost is logged for that sale, it stays honestly blank otherwise rather than assuming either one is $0, and only ever subtracts the one(s) that are actually logged.

Tax / 1099-K threshold tracker

Not tax advice. As of the 2026 tax year, the federal Form 1099-K reporting threshold is back to more than $20,000 in gross payments AND more than 200 transactions, per platform, restored permanently by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (July 2025). A platform only has to send a 1099-K once both numbers are crossed, but every dollar of real profit is taxable income regardless of whether a form gets issued. This reads only real rows from sales.json for the current calendar year, it does not estimate or project future sales.

Business expenses log

Real Schedule C record-keeping, not tax advice. Every dollar here is a real logged expense, or a real mileage entry computed from real miles driven at the actual IRS 2026 standard mileage rate for that date: 72.5¢/mile January 1 through June 30, then 76¢/mile July 1 through December 31 after the IRS's mid-year increase (announced 2026-07-13, driven by fuel prices). An expense with no real amount or miles logged yet shows as such rather than being left out of the count silently, run node public/garage/data/validate.js to find any.
Quick log an expense (generates JSON to paste into expenses.json, saves nothing)

Runs the same checks node public/garage/data/validate.js does on expenses.json (a unique id, a real category), then builds the JSON object to paste into the expenses array yourself. For a mileage expense, leave "Amount" blank and log real miles plus a real date instead, the table above computes the dollar amount itself at the real IRS rate for that date, the same way it already does for a hand-edited entry. This never writes a file or sends anything, it only puts text on your clipboard.
